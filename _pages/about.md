Overview
======
I am a recent graduate of Ursinus College. During my time I was a member of numerous clubs, was awarded a variety of notible distinctions and awards, and graduated top of my class. I have a long-time passion for coding, which was initally spurred on by reading "The Imitation Game: Alan Turing Decoded", written by Jim Ottaviani and illustrated by Leland Purvis, in middle school. This book left me facinated with the concept of machine capable of an artifical form "intelligence" which could, potentially, be able to mimic that of a human. Then in High School, I was introduced to the game "Detroit: Decome Human" developed by Quantic Dream. This game introduced me to the ethical conundrum of where the line begins and ends which separates humans for beings meant to simulate the thought process of humans. In 2022, I started attending Ursinus College, where, under the tutelage of Professors such as [Christopher Tralie](https://www.ctralie.com/) and [William Mongan](https://www.billmongan.com), I learned about Object Oriented Programming, Computer Architecture, AI Ethics, Construction of AI Models, etc. During the semester, I pursued various research opportunities, including in [game design](https://www.ctralie.com/Research/GASPGroup/index.html#students), [sentiment analysis](https://doi.org/10.11648/j.ijdst.20241002.13), and [wireless network security](https://digitalcommons.ursinus.edu/comp_hon/3/). This included a summer in the Mathematics Summer Fellows program, in which I spent two months on campus during the summer break researching text sentiment analysis alongside a team of REU students studying Mathematics, Statistics, and Computer Science. During my time in this program, I was also granted the opportunity to present at the Mathematical Association of America conference in Orlando, Florida. In addition, I also participated in two internships, one with the American Foundation for the Blind learning about digital accessibility and another with the Historic Trappe organization of Trappe, PA researching the history of German-American immigrants in South-East Pennsylvania. When I wasn't maintaining my 4.0 GPA and participating in research and internships, I was an active member of four on-campus groups: Ursinus Rotaract, Ursinus Anime Club, Students Today Alumni Tomorrow, and the Ursinus Chapter of the Alpha Delta Phi Society. Of those groups I was on the executive board for three, playing a notible role in the clubs functioning. As well as being an active and well-respected member of several on-campus organizations, I was also initiated and remain a member in good standing of several prominent discipline-based honor societies including the [Tau Chapter of Phi Beta Kappa](https://www.ursinus.edu/academics/phi-beta-kappa/), and the Ursinus College Modern Languages honor society.
